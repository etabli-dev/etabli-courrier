---
title: "Calendar"
---

Browse cached and local events. Connect Nextcloud in Accounts and Refresh to retrieve calendar resources. Source ICS, recurrence overrides and time-zone identifiers are preserved. A failed resource fetch cannot advance the sync token. Full event editing/import and recurring-reminder expansion are not exposed by the production calendar screen.

See [current implementation and source-build commands](../current-status.qmd). These pages describe the unpublished October 2026 source revision, not the existing development APK.
