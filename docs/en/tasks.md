---
title: "Tasks"
---

Browse local or synced tasks and mark them complete. Completion updates the stored VTODO and its pending upload in one transaction. Repeat-after-completion advances the due date and keeps the task active. Creation/editing screens and full recurring-task scheduling are deferred.

See [current implementation and source-build commands](../current-status.qmd). These pages describe the unpublished October 2026 source revision, not the existing development APK.
