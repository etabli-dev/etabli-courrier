---
title: "DavMail — external integration"
---

DavMail is not bundled or automatically configured. A user-operated bridge may expose IMAP/DAV endpoints, but interoperability, transport security and tenant permissions must be verified independently. This refinement makes no claim that a live DavMail or Exchange installation was tested.

See [current implementation and source-build commands](../current-status.qmd). These pages describe the unpublished October 2026 source revision, not the existing development APK.
